“Onmaum” means “with all our heart.” We are a small giving project that brings warm meals and learning to children in Kenya, and clean water to villages in Malawi.

Who we are

The Onmaum Project was planned by a husband and wife together, and it grows by gathering the goodwill of close friends and acquaintances.

Wanting to give a well in his wife's name, he searched long and carefully for a trustworthy partner, and found GoodMind Foundation (재단법인 선한뜻). He met the foundation's chairperson in person before deciding, and has been deeply satisfied with the people and their work.

Transparency

All donations go directly to GoodMind Foundation's account, never to us personally, and every donor can receive an official donation receipt individually from the foundation.

Malawi wells #1 and #2Funded 100% by the couple's own personal money. No Onmaum donations were used. We named them under the Onmaum Project because that is who we are, and we hope more people will take an interest.
Onmaum donationsDedicated to a single, separate project: warm meals and education for children in Kenya.

Kenya: a meal and a classroom

Together with GoodMind Foundation, Onmaum supports warm school lunches and learning at five Maasai schools in Kenya, reaching about 1,187 children. A meal protects today; education changes tomorrow. Our monthly newsletter reports how every gift is used.

Malawi: Water is Life

Deep borehole wells bring safe water to villages that once relied on shallow, contaminated pits, giving children back the hours they spent carrying water.
